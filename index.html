FAQ

Fair questions.

Why wouldn't we just use AI ourselves?

You could. You could also service your own car: buy the parts, learn the engine, give up your weekend. Most people don't, because it isn't their job and the garage gets it right the first time. We keep up with the tools, we've built the workflows, and a designer signs off every file. You get on with more pressing matters.

Is a person doing this, or a machine?

Both, in that order. A designer works out what makes your reference distinctive, makes the design decisions and signs off every file. AI helps explore layouts and produce variations, which is where the speed comes from.

Can you come up with a new campaign for us?

No, and that's deliberate. A new campaign idea, an identity or a logo is idea work, and it belongs with your lead agency. We start where the idea is already approved.

We don't have our brand guidelines to hand. Is that a problem?

Yes, but it's quickly fixed. Go straight back to your agency and ask for them. We don't guess at a brand or apply it at random. Once we have the guidelines alongside your reference, we can start.

What do you need from us, and when do the 24 hours start?

Four things: the reference, your brand guidelines, final copy and the specifications for each piece. The clock starts once the brief is agreed and all four are in hand, and it runs on working days.

What if we want changes?

One round of changes is included in the fee and comes back within another 24 hours. If you expect to need more, we agree that in the quote.

Will our agency mind?

We don't do idea work, so we aren't after their account. We take the jobs that are too small for an agency studio and too visible for PowerPoint. Agencies can also brief us themselves and deliver the work under their own name.

Is our brand material safe with you?

Yes. Our designers work only in tools the studio has approved, set so that your material is not used to train AI models. Nothing we make for you is shown publicly without your permission.

Who owns the finished artwork?

You do, once it's paid for. We keep a copy in your brand file so that your next brief starts from what's already approved.
