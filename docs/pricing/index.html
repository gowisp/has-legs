Pricing questions

About the fee.

All the questions

How is the fee worked out?

It's a fixed fee, agreed before we start. It reflects how much new design is needed, the number of formats and the changes we've agreed. A Single starts from R2,400, and each further piece in a Set from R1,800.

Why do your prices say "from"?

Because a pocket scorecard and a stage backdrop aren't the same amount of design. The "from" price is where a straightforward piece starts. You get the exact fee, fixed, before the clock starts.

What if we want changes?

One round of changes is included in the fee and comes back within another 24 hours. If you expect to need more, we agree that in the quote.

Do you do resizes?

Not on their own. A resize refits an existing layout to new dimensions, with no design decisions to make. When resizes are part of a Set or a Kit, they come included.

What about bigger jobs?

A Kit covers everything for one event or campaign, so it gets its own timing. We quote within one working day and give you a delivery date with the quote.

Got work that has legs?

Send us the reference, your brand guidelines and what you need next. We'll come back with a fixed fee and a time.
