Agency questions

Asked and answered.

All the questions

Will you approach our clients?

No. We never approach an agency's client about work the agency brought in, and idea work always goes back to the lead agency. If a client of yours finds us on their own, we take the brief, and the idea work still goes back to you.

We're an agency. Can we put our name on it?

Yes. Brief us yourself and deliver the work under your own name. You manage the client relationship, and your client never sees us.

Can you come up with a new campaign for us?

No, and that's deliberate. A new campaign idea, an identity or a logo is idea work, and it belongs with your lead agency. We start where the idea is already approved.

Who owns the finished artwork?

You do, once it's paid for. We keep a copy in your brand file so that your next brief starts from what's already approved.

Will you show our work to other people?

Not without your permission. That's why the work on this site is demonstrations on invented brands.

Got work that has legs?

Send us the reference, your brand guidelines and what you need next. We'll come back with a fixed fee and a time.
