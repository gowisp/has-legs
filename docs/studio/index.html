The studio

Good work deserves more mileage.

Has Legs is a creative extension studio. We exist for the part of the job that comes after the idea is approved: making everything that follows look like it belongs.

Why we exist

Work gets approved, loved, and then used once.

A brand spends months getting a piece of creative right. Then the golf day arrives, or the conference, or the in-store promotion, and the pieces for it get made in a hurry by whoever is free. The idea was strong enough to go further. Nobody had the time to take it there. That is the gap we fill.

How we're built

Designer-led, AI-powered workflows, designed for scale.

01

Designer-led

A designer directs every job. They choose between the routes the pipeline offers, send work back with notes, or take a piece over by hand. Nothing reaches you until they've signed it off.

02

AI-powered

We built our own AI pipeline for this one job. It reads your reference and your brand guidelines, builds each piece and exports the finished files. Where a piece needs a picture, it generates one.

03

Designed for scale

Scale, for us, is one reference carried across everything you need at once: a map, eighteen hole signs, wayfinding and scorecards, all in step. Your creative goes further without losing its thread.

Before you see anything

Every piece passes three checks.

A piece that fails any one of them goes back to be rebuilt. And every fault we catch becomes a permanent check, so the same mistake doesn't come back.

  1. StartThe pipeline builds the piece
  2. 01

    Measured

    Size, bleed, colour, resolution and fonts are measured, and every word is matched against your job sheet. Each one is a plain pass or fail.

  3. 02

    A fresh eye

    A separate reviewer that never saw the piece being made compares it with your reference, your signed tearsheet and the job sheet. It has to show evidence for every fault.

  4. 03

    The designer

    A person looks last. If it isn't right, they direct a redo or finish the piece by hand. Then they sign it off.

  5. ThenThe proof goes to you

How we use AI, and your work

Five things we hold to.

What a brand team should know before it hands over its work. Stated up front.

  • A designer signs off every file. The pipeline builds. A person makes the calls and puts their name to the result.
  • Your material doesn't train AI. The AI services we build with are set so that your material is not used to train their models.
  • We tell you what was generated. Where a piece uses an AI-generated picture, the proof says which ones, so you approve them knowingly.
  • Your artwork is yours. The finished artwork belongs to you once it's paid for.
  • Nothing is shown without permission. We don't show client work publicly unless the client says we can. That's why the work on this site is demonstrations on invented brands.

Why wouldn't we just use AI ourselves?

You could. You could also service your own car: buy the parts, learn the engine, give up your weekend. Most people don't, because it isn't their job and the garage gets it right the first time.

We keep up with the tools, we've built the workflows, and a designer signs off every file. You get on with more pressing matters.
