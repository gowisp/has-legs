Client eventDemonstration: invented brand

One deck. One golf day. Same handwriting.

Verlane Bank has a presentation template it loves and a client golf day coming up. Six designs for one afternoon, and a golfer has to read the arrow and the hole number from a moving cart.

What carried across

  • The single-weight line Hills on the title slide, fairways on the map, arrows on the signs.
  • The yellow dot The sun in the deck. Every pin, and the start of every arrow.
  • The serif Headlines in the deck. Hole numbers you can read at speed.
  • The palette Navy, paper and one accent. Nothing new invited in.

Retail campaignDemonstration: invented brand

A campaign poster becomes the in-store set.

Tumble Soda's summer poster is approved and up. The shops need a shelf strip, a wobbler and a counter card to match. More pieces, same campaign.

What carried across

  • The tumble The can always falls at the same angle.
  • The yellow circle The sun behind the can, every time.
  • The bubbles Loose circles, never in a row.
  • The wave edge Every piece ends on it.
  • The heavy, rounded type One family, two weights, no exceptions.

Internal eventDemonstration: invented brand

A report's illustration style becomes a conference.

Okavi Energy's integrated report has a look the executive team is proud of. A report is read at arm's length and a stage from the back row, so the style has to make that jump without turning into something else.

What carried across

  • The rising arcs A cover illustration, a sunrise behind the speaker, a corner of the agenda.
  • The stacked bars Under the cover's horizon, under the badge's arcs, at the foot of the agenda.
  • The palette The stage goes green where the report was sand. The three colours don't change.
  • The type The report's headline face, from a cover title to a name on a lanyard.
