A report's illustration style becomes a conference.

Okavi Energy's integrated report has a look the executive team is proud of. The staff conference is in three weeks. Same style, a different medium.

01 The reference

A style made for the page, asked to fill a room.

A report is read at arm's length. A stage is read from the back row. The style has to make that jump without turning into something else.

  • The rising arcs Concentric, always sitting on a horizon.
  • The stacked bars The arcs' reflection, getting shorter.
  • Green, terracotta, sand Three colours, in flat blocks.
  • A wide, geometric sans Short lines, set tight.

02 The brief

Three weeks out, three pieces.

The conference theme is written and signed off. The venue has sent the stage dimensions. What's missing is everything people will actually look at on the day.

The brief Staff conference 2027

What we need next
  • Stage backdrop 1
  • Name badges 1 design, 240 names
  • Agenda card 1
Where they'll be used
On stage, on lanyards and on every seat.
By when
With the venue and the printer two weeks before the day.

03 The extensions

Same style, a different medium.

What carried across

  • The rising arcs A cover illustration, a sunrise behind the speaker, a corner of the agenda.
  • The stacked bars Under the cover's horizon, under the badge's arcs, at the foot of the agenda.
  • The palette The stage goes green where the report was sand. The three colours don't change.
  • The type The report's headline face, from a cover title to a name on a lanyard.

04 The edges

What we left alone.

  • A new theme"The next ten years" came from Okavi, signed off. We set it. We didn't write it.
  • A new colourGreen, terracotta and sand, exactly as the report has them.
  • A new styleAn illustration style for next year's report would be idea work. That goes back to the lead agency.

Set

Three related extensions from one reference. That makes this job a Set: a fixed fee from R6,000 for three pieces, first finished artwork in 24 hours and one round of changes included.
