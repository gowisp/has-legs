Privacy

Your material, your business.

What this website collects, where your brief goes, and where the material you send us travels. In plain words.

This website

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers. Its fonts and scripts are served from the site itself, so opening a page doesn't call out to anyone else.

Like any website, the company that hosts it keeps standard server logs of visits.

The brief builder

What you type into the brief builder stays in your own browser. It is saved on your device so that a refresh doesn't lose your work, and "Clear the form and start again" deletes it.

Nothing reaches us until you send the brief yourself, from your own email.

What you send us

When you email a brief, we use it and the files that come with it to agree the job and to do it. That means your reference, your brand guidelines, your copy and your specifications.

To build your pieces, that material passes through the AI services we work with. We keep it, with your finished files, in your brand file, so that your next brief starts from it.

What we promise about all of this is on the Studio page, under five things we hold to.

Questions
