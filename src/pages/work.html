How to read a demonstration

Three things to look for.

  1. The reference

    The approved work the client already loves. It goes in untouched.

  2. The creative language

    What makes the reference distinctive: its line, its colour, its type, its layout. This is what has to carry.

  3. The extensions

    New pieces, with new design decisions, that speak the same language. No new idea anywhere.
