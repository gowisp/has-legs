How it works

From brief to finished artwork in a day.

Four steps, one designer, and a clock that starts when you're ready. Here is exactly what happens after you send a brief, and what we need from you to keep it to 24 hours.

{{> mascot/clock.svg}}

  1. Send the brief

    You

    Tell us what you need next: which pieces, where they'll be used, and by when. The brief builder walks you through it in about five minutes.

    We come back with a fixed fee. If part of the job is idea work, we'll say so and point it back to your lead agency.

  2. Get four things to us

    You

    The reference, your brand guidelines, final copy and the specifications. The clock starts when the brief is agreed and all four are in hand.

    If one is missing, the clock waits. It doesn't run against you.

  3. One designer takes it through

    Us

    The same designer handles your brief, your feedback and the handover. First they work out what makes your reference distinctive. Then they design the new pieces in that same creative language.

    AI does the exploring: more layout routes, more variations, more formats. The designer makes the calls.

  4. Artwork in 24 hours

    Us, then you

    On working days, for a Single or a Set. Every file is checked against your specifications and signed off by the designer before it reaches you.

    One round of changes is included and comes back within another 24 hours. A Kit gets a delivery date with its quote.

The 24-hour standard

A clock you can plan around.

First finished artwork within 24 hours of an agreed brief. Here is the whole of it, with nothing in small print.

  1. The start Brief agreed All four things in hand. The clock starts.
  2. Within 24 hours First finished artwork Designed, checked against your specifications and signed off.
  3. In your own time Your changes One round, included in the fee. The clock waits for you.
  4. Within another 24 hours Final files Changes made, checked and signed off again.
  • Working daysThe clock doesn't run on weekends or public holidays.
  • Singles and SetsA Kit is bigger, so it gets a delivery date with its quote.
  • One round includedIf you expect to need more, we agree that in the quote.

What we need from you

The clock starts with four things.

Have these ready and the 24 hours are ours to keep. Miss one and the clock simply waits until it arrives.

{{> icon-tick.svg}}

The reference

The approved work you already love. It sets the creative language for everything we make.

A file or a link is fine.

{{> icon-tick.svg}}

Your brand guidelines

Your brand's own rules for logo, colour and type. We don't guess at a brand or apply it at random.

Not to hand? Your lead agency has them.

{{> icon-tick.svg}}

Final copy

Every word that goes on the pieces, signed off on your side before we start.

Final copy is what keeps it to a day.

{{> icon-tick.svg}}

Specifications

The sizes, bleed and file formats for each piece, so the artwork is right the first time.

Ask your printer or venue.

Is my job an extension?

New design decisions, no new idea.

That's the test. If the look is already approved and you need more made in it, send it to us. If the look itself is the question, that's a job for your lead agency.

Send it to us

  • More pieces for a campaign that's already approved
  • An approved style carried into a different medium
  • Signs, print and screens for an event with an approved look
  • Resizes that come along with a Set or a Kit

Send it elsewhere

  • A new campaign idea Idea work. Back to your lead agency.
  • A new identity or logo Idea work. Back to your lead agency.
  • A resize on its own We only do resizes inside a Set or a Kit.
  • A job with no brand guidelines Get them from your agency first, then come back.
