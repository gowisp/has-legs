How it works

Five steps. Two sign-offs.

Here is what happens after you send a brief. You sign off twice: once on the look, before we build everything, and once on the finished work.

{{> mascot/clock.svg}}
  1. You send Brief
  2. We reply Job sheet
  3. You sign off the look Tearsheet
  4. You sign off the work Proof
  5. Yours to use Files

  1. Send the brief

    You

    Pick what you need from the catalogue and tell us where each piece will be used. Send what you have with it: the reference, your brand guidelines, the copy and the specifications.

    The brief builder walks you through it in about five minutes.

  2. We reply with a job sheet

    Us, then you

    One page that lists every piece with its size, use, copy and specification, plus the fixed fee, the delivery time and your approver. Anything not on it is not in the job.

    If something is missing, we send one list of what we still need. You accept the job sheet and pay, and we start.

  3. You sign off the look

    Us, then you

    Before we build everything, we send a tearsheet: one designed page that shows how we read your brand and your reference, with one finished piece from the job.

    Changes are free until you sign it. Once signed, it is the standard the rest of the job is built to.

  4. We build the rest and send a proof

    Us, then you

    Every piece is built, checked and passed by the designer before you see it. The three checks are on the Studio page.

    The proof comes by email to your approver, who sends back one list of changes.

  5. Approve, and the files are yours

    You

    Your approver approves in writing and we send the final files, each in the format it needs: a print-ready PDF for print, the open file for a PowerPoint.

    Want the open files for anything else? Ask, and they're yours.

What we need from you

A brief with nothing missing.

A brief is ready when every piece has a size, a use, final copy and a specification. Four things get it there.

{{> icon-tick.svg}}

The reference

The approved work you already love. It sets the creative language for everything we make.

A file or a link is fine.

{{> icon-tick.svg}}

Your brand guidelines

Your brand's own rules for logo, colour and type. We ask for them with every brief, because we don't guess at a brand.

Not to hand? Your lead agency has them.

{{> icon-tick.svg}}

Final copy

Every word that goes on the pieces, signed off on your side before we start.

Final means final: new copy later is an addition.

{{> icon-tick.svg}}

Specifications

The sizes, bleed and file formats for each piece, so the artwork is right the first time.

Ask your printer or venue.

No brand guidelines at all?

We'd much rather start from guidelines, and most brands have them somewhere. If yours has none, say so in the brief. We write the rules down ourselves from your website and your reference, and you see and sign them on the tearsheet before anything else is built.

How changes work

One person. One list.

Every job names one approver on your side. They gather everyone's comments and send them as a single list. Comments from anyone else go back to the approver.

Everything on that list is one of three things. The test is whether the brief changed, or only our answer to it.

Correction

We got something wrong against the job sheet, your copy, the specifications or your brand guidelines. Our mistake, so we fix it.

Free, never counted

Change

The brief is the same and you'd like our answer adjusted: the layout, the emphasis, the choice of image, a colour within your palette.

Included in the fee

Addition

The brief itself has changed: another piece, new copy, or a different look from the tearsheet you signed. We tell you the fee and the time first, and start when you say yes.

Priced before we start it

What approval means

Read the proof like it's the print run.

  • In writingFinal files go out only once your approver has said yes in writing.
  • Then it's yoursIf a mistake was visible in the proof you approved, the cost of reprinting is yours.
  • Ours stay oursWe still correct our own mistakes in the files, at no charge.

Hours, not days

We measure turnaround on every job: the time from an agreed brief to your first proof. Our side moves in hours.

The two sign-offs are yours, so how fast the whole job goes is partly in your hands.

The second brief is quicker than the first

Your signed tearsheet, your approved files and the decisions behind them go into your brand file.

Your next brief on the same reference starts from there, and skips the first sign-off.
