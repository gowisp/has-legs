Privacy

Your material, your business.

What this website collects, where your brief goes, and what we do with the work you send us. In plain words.

{{> mascot/lock.svg}}

This website

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers. Its fonts and scripts are served from the site itself, so opening a page doesn't call out to anyone else.

Like any website, the company that hosts it keeps standard server logs of visits.

The brief builder

What you type into the brief builder stays in your own browser. It is saved on your device so that a refresh doesn't lose your work, and "Clear the form and start again" deletes it.

Nothing reaches us until you send the brief yourself, from your own email.

What you send us

When you email a brief, we use it and the files that come with it to quote and to do the job. That means your reference, your brand guidelines, your copy and your specifications.

  • Our designers work only in tools the studio has approved, set so that your material is not used to train AI models.
  • Nothing we make for you is shown publicly without your permission.
  • The finished artwork is yours once it's paid for.
  • We keep your approved assets and the design decisions from each job in your brand file, so that your next brief starts from them.

Questions

If you want to know what we hold for you, or you have a question about any of this, write to {{site.email}}.
