01 The reference

A poster with plenty to say.

Tumble's poster is loud on purpose. Five things make it Tumble, and all five have to survive the trip to a shelf edge 30 millimetres tall.

  • The tumble The can always falls at the same angle.
  • The yellow circle The sun behind the can, every time.
  • The bubbles Loose circles, never in a row.
  • The wave edge Every piece ends on it.
  • The heavy, rounded type One family, two weights, no exceptions.

02 The brief
