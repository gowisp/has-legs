Send a brief

Tell us what you need next.

Six short sections, about five minutes. We reply with a job sheet: every piece, a fixed fee and a delivery time. Nothing you type leaves this page until you send it yourself, from your own email.

{{> mascot/envelope.svg}}

01 About you

Where do you sit?
How would you like to work?

One person gathers everyone's comments and signs off.

02 The reference

The approved work that sets the look: a deck, a poster, a report, an event identity.

No link? Attach the files to your email at the end.

03 Brand guidelines

Your brand's own rules for logo, colour and type. We ask for them alongside the reference.

Do you have them?

Ask your lead agency for them. They'll have them on file. You can send the brief now and the guidelines when they arrive.

We can still start. We'd much rather work from guidelines. Where a brand has none, we write the rules down ourselves from your website and your reference, and you sign them off before we build anything else.

04 What you need next

List each piece. Pick it from the catalogue or type your own, then say its size and where it will be used.

    05 Copy and specifications

    Is the copy final?
    Do you have the specifications?

    Specifications are the sizes, bleed and file formats your printer, venue or platform asks for.
