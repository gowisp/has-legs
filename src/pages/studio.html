The studio

Good work deserves more mileage.

Has Legs is a creative extension studio. We exist for the part of the job that comes after the idea is approved: making everything that follows look like it belongs.

{{> mascot/pencil.svg}}

Why we exist

Work gets approved, loved, and then used once.

A brand spends months getting a piece of creative right. Then the golf day arrives, or the conference, or the in-store promotion, and the pieces for it get made in a hurry by whoever is free. The idea was strong enough to go further. Nobody had the time to take it there.

That's the gap we fill. Bring us the work you already love and a brief for what you need next. We work out what makes it distinctive and design the new pieces in that same creative language. The original goes further, and the new work looks like it was always part of the plan.

How we're built

Designer-led, AI-assisted workflows, designed for scale.

01

Designer-led

A designer works out what makes your reference distinctive, makes every design decision and signs off every file. One designer takes each job from brief to handover.

02

AI-assisted

We built our production workflows around AI from the ground up. It explores more layout routes, tests more variations and rolls a design across more formats than a traditional studio could manage in the same day.

03

Designed for scale

Scale, for us, is one reference carried across a whole kit at once: a map, eighteen hole signs, wayfinding and scorecards, all in step. Your creative goes further without losing its thread.

How we use AI, and your work

Six things we hold to.

What a brand team should know before it hands over its work. Stated up front.

  • {{> icon-tick.svg}} A designer signs off every file. AI helps us explore and produce. A person makes the calls and puts their name to the result.
  • {{> icon-tick.svg}} Approved tools only. Our designers work only in tools the studio has approved, set so that your material is not used to train AI models.
  • {{> icon-tick.svg}} Your artwork is yours. The finished artwork belongs to you once it's paid for. We keep a copy in your brand file so your next brief starts from it.
  • {{> icon-tick.svg}} Nothing is shown without permission. We don't show client work publicly unless the client says we can. That's why the work on this site is demonstrations on invented brands.
  • {{> icon-tick.svg}} We don't guess at a brand. No brand guidelines, no start. It keeps your brand from being applied at random.
  • {{> icon-tick.svg}} Idea work goes back to your agency. We don't do campaigns, identities or logos, and we aren't after anyone's account.

Who does the work

Has Legs works with a pool of designers, not one name on the door. Every job gets one designer from brief to handover.

Your brand file carries the memory between jobs, so whichever designer picks up your next brief starts from what you've already approved.

Where we work

We're based in South Africa. We quote in rand, and the 24-hour clock runs on South African working days.
