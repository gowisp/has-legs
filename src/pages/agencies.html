For agencies

Keep the idea. Hand off the legwork.

Follow-on work is good for the account and hard on the studio. We take it on while your team develops the next idea.

Two ways to work

Send it on, or put your name on it.

We handle the technical side of bringing AI to creative. You focus on being creative.

Referral

Refer it

Pass on the jobs that are too small to be worth your studio's time. We work with your client's brand team directly, in the creative language you set.

Your agency Has Legs Their brand team
  1. You point your client our way, or send the brief on.
  2. We agree a fixed fee with the brand team and do the work.
  3. Anything that needs a new idea comes straight back to you.
White-label

White-label it

Brief us yourself and deliver the work under your own name. You manage the client relationship. Your client never sees us.

Your client Your agency Has Legs
  1. You send the brief, with the reference and the brand guidelines.
  2. We quote you a fixed fee and deliver finished artwork to you.
  3. You deliver it to your client under your own name.
One rule

Idea work always goes back to you, and we never approach your client about work you brought in.

If a client of yours finds us on their own, we take the brief. The idea work still goes back to you.

What stays yours

You keep what matters.

  • The idea We start where the idea is already approved, and we stop where a new one is needed.
  • The relationship White-labelled, your client never sees us. Referred, we never approach them about work you brought in.
  • Your studio's time Your team develops the next idea while the follow-on work gets done.
